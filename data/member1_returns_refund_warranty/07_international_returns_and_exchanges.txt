Document: International Returns and Exchanges
Owner: Team Member 1
============================================================

ELIGIBLE REGIONS
NovaTech ships to the US, Canada, UK, EU and Australia. Returns follow the same 30-day
window in all regions.

CUSTOMS AND DUTIES
Customers are responsible for return shipping costs on non-defective international
returns. Import duties paid at delivery are refunded only if the carrier refunds NovaTech.

EXCHANGES
Exchanges for a different model are processed as a return plus a new order. NovaTech holds
the new item for 5 days if requested at the time of the RMA.

VOLTAGE AND PLUG TYPES
Devices ordered for one region may not work in another. Smart plugs and the NovaHub power
adapter are region-specific and are not eligible for exchange due to incompatibility.

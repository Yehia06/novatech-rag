Document: Returns, Refunds and Warranty FAQ
Owner: Team Member 1
============================================================

Q: CAN I RETURN A DEVICE AFTER 30 DAYS?
No, except for warranty claims. Defective items are handled under warranty.

Q: DO I NEED THE ORIGINAL BOX?
Yes. Without it a 15 percent restocking fee applies.

Q: HOW LONG UNTIL MY REFUND ARRIVES?
Approximately 7 to 13 business days from the day NovaTech receives the item.

Q: DOES THE WARRANTY TRANSFER IF I SELL MY DEVICE?
The warranty is transferable once. The new owner must register the serial number within 30
days.

Q: IS A BOUGHT-ON-AMAZON DEVICE COVERED?
Yes, if purchased from an authorized retailer, with a valid receipt.

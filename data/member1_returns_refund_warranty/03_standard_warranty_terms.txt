COVERAGE PERIOD
All NovaTech hardware carries a 12-month limited warranty starting on the delivery date.
NovaLock Pro and NovaStat Learning thermostat carry 24 months.

WHAT IS COVERED
Manufacturing defects in materials and workmanship under normal household use, including
battery failures below 60 percent of rated capacity within the first 12 months.

WHAT IS NOT COVERED
Physical damage, water damage beyond the stated IP rating, unauthorized repairs or
firmware modification, power surges, and cosmetic wear such as scratches.

REMEDY
NovaTech will at its option repair, replace with a new or refurbished unit, or refund the
purchase price. Replacement units are warranted for the remainder of the original period
or 90 days, whichever is longer.

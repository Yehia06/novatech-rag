NOVATECH SMART HOME - SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE
Document: Defective on Arrival (DOA) Policy
Owner: Team Member 1
============================================================

DEFINITION
A device is DOA if it fails to power on or fails basic function within the first 14 days
after delivery.

RESOLUTION
DOA devices are replaced at no cost with expedited 2-day shipping, or fully refunded
including original shipping, at the customer's choice.

REQUIREMENTS
Customers must report DOA issues within 14 days. Support may request a short video showing
the fault, such as the status LED behavior.

SHIPPING DAMAGE
If the box arrived visibly damaged, customers should photograph the packaging before
opening it. Shipping-damage claims are handled as DOA.

NOVATECH SMART HOME - SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE
Document: How to File a Warranty Claim
Owner: Team Member 1
============================================================

STEP 1: VERIFY
Confirm the device serial number in the NovaTech app under Device > About. Serial numbers
begin with NT followed by 10 digits.

STEP 2: TROUBLESHOOT
Support agents will first walk through the relevant troubleshooting guide and a factory
reset to rule out configuration issues.

STEP 3: SUBMIT
Provide proof of purchase, serial number, a short description, and photos or video of the
fault. Claims are reviewed within 2 business days.

STEP 4: RESOLUTION
Approved claims receive a prepaid shipping label. Advance replacement is available to
NovaCare Plus members only; all other customers receive replacements after the faulty unit
is received.

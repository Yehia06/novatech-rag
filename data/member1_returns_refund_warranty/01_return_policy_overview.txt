NOVATECH SMART HOME - SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE
Document: NovaTech Return Policy Overview
Owner: Team Member 1
============================================================

SCOPE
This policy applies to all NovaTech Smart Home hardware purchased directly from novatech-
home.example or an authorized NovaTech retailer. Subscriptions and digital services are
covered in the Cloud Plans documents.

RETURN WINDOW
Customers may return unused or lightly used devices within 30 days of the delivery date.
The 30-day period starts on the delivery date shown on the carrier tracking page, not the
order date.

CONDITION REQUIREMENTS
The device must include all original accessories, cables, mounting hardware and packaging.
Devices must be factory reset and removed from the customer's NovaTech account before
return.

NON-RETURNABLE ITEMS
Opened software license keys, gift cards, and devices damaged by misuse or liquid are not
eligible for return.

HOW TO START
Open a return request in the NovaTech app under Support > Returns, or contact support. A
Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number is issued within 1 business day.

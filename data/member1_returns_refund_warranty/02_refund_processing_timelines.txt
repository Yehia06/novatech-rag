Document: Refund Processing Timelines
Owner: Team Member 1
============================================================

OVERVIEW
Refunds are issued to the original payment method only.

TIMELINE
After the returned device arrives at the NovaTech warehouse, inspection takes 2 to 3
business days. Once approved, the refund is submitted immediately and appears within 5 to
10 business days depending on the bank.

PARTIAL REFUNDS
A 15 percent restocking fee applies to opened devices missing original packaging. Devices
missing accessories are refunded minus the replacement cost of the missing items.

SHIPPING COSTS
Original shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to a NovaTech error or
defect. Return shipping is free for defective items using the prepaid label in the RMA
email.

REFUND NOT RECEIVED
If the refund has not arrived after 10 business days, customers should first check with
their bank, then contact support with the RMA number.

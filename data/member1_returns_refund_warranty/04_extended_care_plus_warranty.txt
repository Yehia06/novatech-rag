Document: NovaCare Plus Extended Warranty
Owner: Team Member 1
============================================================

OVERVIEW
NovaCare Plus extends hardware coverage and adds accidental damage protection.

PRICING
NovaCare Plus costs 4.99 USD per month per device or 49 USD per year per device. Hubs are
7.99 USD per month.

COVERAGE
Adds up to 36 months total coverage, two accidental damage claims per 12 months, and
priority replacement with advance shipping.

DEDUCTIBLE
Accidental damage claims carry a 29 USD deductible. Defect claims have no deductible.

ENROLLMENT
Plans must be purchased within 60 days of device delivery. The plan can be cancelled any
time; unused annual fees are refunded pro rata.

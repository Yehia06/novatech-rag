NOVATECH SMART HOME - SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE
Document: Local microSD Storage and Error C305
Owner: Team Member 3
============================================================

SUPPORTED CARDS
microSD Class 10 / U1, 8 GB to 256 GB, formatted FAT32 or exFAT.

RECORDING BEHAVIOR
Continuous recording overwrites oldest files when full. A 128 GB card holds roughly 14
days at 1080p.

ERROR C305
Error C305 means SD card unreadable or corrupted. Format the card in the app under Camera
> Storage > Format. If the error persists, use a high-endurance card.

TIP
Remove the card only after powering off the camera to prevent corruption.

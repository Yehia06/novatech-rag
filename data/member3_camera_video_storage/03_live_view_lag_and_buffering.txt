NOVATECH SMART HOME - SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE
Document: Live View Lag and Buffering
Owner: Team Member 3
============================================================

COMMON CAUSES
Low upload bandwidth, too many simultaneous streams, or a congested 2.4 GHz channel.

SOLUTIONS
Lower the stream quality in Camera Settings > Video Quality (Auto, 1080p, 720p). Limit
live view to 2 viewers at a time. Switch the router to channel 1, 6 or 11.

BANDWIDTH TABLE
720p uses roughly 1 Mbps, 1080p about 2 Mbps, 2K about 4 Mbps per camera.

LATENCY
Typical live view delay is 2 to 4 seconds. Delays above 10 seconds indicate network
problems.

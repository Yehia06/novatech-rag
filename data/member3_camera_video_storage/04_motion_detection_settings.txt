Document: Motion Detection and Alerts
Owner: Team Member 3
============================================================

SENSITIVITY
Three levels: Low, Medium, High. Outdoor cameras should usually use Low or Medium to avoid
false alerts from trees and headlights.

ACTIVITY ZONES
Draw up to 4 zones in the app to ignore streets or neighbors' yards.

PERSON AND PACKAGE DETECTION
Available with the Cloud Plus plan. Free accounts receive generic motion alerts only.

TOO MANY ALERTS
Lower sensitivity, add activity zones, set a 5-minute cooldown, or schedule quiet hours.

NO ALERTS
Check notification permissions on the phone, battery saver mode, and that the camera
schedule is Armed.

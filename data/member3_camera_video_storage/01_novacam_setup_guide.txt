NOVATECH SMART HOME - SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE
Document: NovaCam Setup Guide
Owner: Team Member 3
============================================================

MODELS
NovaCam Indoor (1080p), NovaCam Outdoor (2K, IP65), NovaCam Doorbell (2K, battery).

PAIRING
Power on the camera and wait for the voice prompt Ready to connect. In the app choose Add
Device > NovaCam and scan the QR code on the camera. Connect to a 2.4 GHz network.

MOUNTING
Outdoor cameras should be mounted 2.5 to 3 meters high, angled downward 20 degrees, and
under an eave to reduce rain on the lens.

FIRST RUN
Allow 2 minutes for the first firmware update. Do not power-cycle during this time.

============================================================

INFRARED
Indoor and outdoor cameras use 850 nm infrared LEDs with range up to 8 meters.

GLARE PROBLEMS
Reflective surfaces such as windows cause white-out. Move the camera away from glass or
disable IR and use the porch light.

FOGGY IMAGE
Spiders and condensation can fog the lens. Wipe with a microfiber cloth. Outdoor models
contain a desiccant pouch and should not be opened.

COLOR NIGHT MODE
Available on 2K models; requires at least 5 lux ambient light.

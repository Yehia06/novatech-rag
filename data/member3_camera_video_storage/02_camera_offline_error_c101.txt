NOVATECH SMART HOME - SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE
Document: Camera Offline: Error C101
Owner: Team Member 3
============================================================

SYMPTOM
Live view shows Camera Offline and the app displays Error C101.

CAUSES
Weak Wi-Fi signal, router change, power loss, or expired DHCP lease.

FIX
1. Check the camera power LED. 2. Move the camera closer to the router or add Ethernet via
the NovaCam Wired Adapter. 3. Reboot the router. 4. Re-add the camera if the Wi-Fi
password was changed.

ESCALATION
If the camera stays offline after reset, collect the serial number and contact support for
diagnostic logs.

Document: Cloud Video Storage
Owner: Team Member 3
============================================================

FREE TIER
Free accounts get 3 days of motion clips, up to 10 seconds each.

CLOUD PLUS
Cloud Plus provides 30 days of clip history and up to 3 minutes per event.

CLOUD PRO
Cloud Pro offers 60 days of continuous 24/7 recording for up to 5 cameras. Pricing is in
the Cloud Plans document.

RETENTION
Clips older than the retention period are deleted automatically and cannot be recovered.
Users can download clips before expiry.

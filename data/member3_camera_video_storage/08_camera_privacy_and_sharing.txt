Document: Camera Privacy and Sharing
Owner: Team Member 3
============================================================

PRIVACY MODE
Enabling Privacy Mode physically covers the lens on indoor models and stops recording and
streaming.

SHARING ACCESS
Owners can invite up to 10 members with Viewer or Manager roles. Viewers cannot delete
clips.

ENCRYPTION
Video is encrypted with AES-256 in transit and at rest.

LAW ENFORCEMENT
NovaTech discloses video only with a valid legal order or with the owner's consent.

Document: NovaStat Thermostat Installation and Wiring
Owner: Team Member 4
============================================================

COMPATIBILITY
Works with most 24V systems: gas, electric, heat pump, and dual fuel. Not compatible with
120V line voltage baseboard heaters.

C-WIRE
A common (C) wire is required. If missing, install the included Power Extender Kit.

WIRING LABELS
Label each wire before disconnecting. Terminals: R, W, Y, G, C, O/B, and Rc.

SETUP
Turn off power at the breaker. Mount the base, connect wires, snap on the display, restore
power, and follow the on-screen setup.

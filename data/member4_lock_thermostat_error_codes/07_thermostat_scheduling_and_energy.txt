Owner: Team Member 4
============================================================

LEARNING MODE
NovaStat Learning adapts after about 7 days of manual adjustments.

SCHEDULES
Up to 6 periods per day, per weekday.

GEOFENCING
Switches to Away mode when all phones leave a 1 km radius.

SAVINGS
NovaTech estimates average savings of 10 to 12 percent on heating and 15 percent on
cooling, depending on climate and usage.

ECO TEMPERATURES
Default Away heat is 16 C (61 F) and cool is 28 C (82 F).

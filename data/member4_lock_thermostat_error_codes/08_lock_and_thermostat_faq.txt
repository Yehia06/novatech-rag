Q: CAN NOVALOCK BE HACKED OVER BLUETOOTH?
Communication uses AES-128 encrypted pairing and rolling keys.

Q: DOES NOVALOCK WORK DURING AN OUTAGE?
Yes. It runs on batteries and keypad access continues to work.

Q: WHY IS MY THERMOSTAT 2 DEGREES OFF?
Calibrate the offset in Settings > Temperature Offset (range plus or minus 3 degrees).

Q: CAN NOVASTAT CONTROL HUMIDIFIERS?
Yes, via the accessory wire H on supported models.

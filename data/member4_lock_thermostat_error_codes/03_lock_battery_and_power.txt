NOVATECH SMART HOME - SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE
Document: NovaLock Battery and Emergency Power
Owner: Team Member 4
============================================================

BATTERY LIFE
About 8 to 12 months with typical use of 10 operations per day.

WARNINGS
The app alerts at 20 percent; keypad flashes red at 10 percent.

EMERGENCY POWER
If batteries die, touch a 9V battery to the two contacts at the bottom of the keypad to
wake it and enter your PIN.

PHYSICAL KEY
A backup key is supplied with every lock. Store one outside the house securely.

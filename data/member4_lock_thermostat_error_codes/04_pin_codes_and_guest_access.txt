NOVATECH SMART HOME - SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE
Document: PIN Codes and Guest Access
Owner: Team Member 4
============================================================

PIN RULES
PINs are 4 to 8 digits. Sequences like 1234 are rejected.

CAPACITY
Up to 100 unique PIN codes.

TEMPORARY CODES
Owners can set a start and end time, or a one-time code. Requires the hub to be online to
sync.

AUTO-LOCK
Configurable delay from 10 seconds to 10 minutes. Default is 30 seconds.

ACTIVITY LOG
The last 500 events are stored with user names.

Document: NovaLock Pro Installation
Owner: Team Member 4
============================================================

COMPATIBILITY
Fits standard US deadbolts with door thickness 35 to 57 mm and 2-1/8 inch bore.

TOOLS
Phillips screwdriver and 4 AA batteries (included).

STEPS
Remove the old deadbolt, install the latch, mount the exterior assembly, route the cable,
install the interior unit, insert batteries, and run Calibrate Door in the app.

DOOR ALIGNMENT
If the bolt does not fully extend, adjust the strike plate. Misalignment is the most
common cause of jamming (Error L203).

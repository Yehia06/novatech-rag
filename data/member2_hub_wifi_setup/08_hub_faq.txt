Q: HOW MANY DEVICES CAN ONE HUB SUPPORT?
Up to 64 devices, including 8 cameras.

Q: DOES THE HUB WORK WITHOUT INTERNET?
Local automations and lock control over Bluetooth continue to work. Remote access, cloud
video and voice assistants require internet.

Q: CAN I USE TWO HUBS?
Yes, up to 3 hubs per account, each with its own device list.

Q: WHICH VOICE ASSISTANTS ARE SUPPORTED?
Amazon Alexa, Google Assistant and Apple HomeKit (via the hub only).

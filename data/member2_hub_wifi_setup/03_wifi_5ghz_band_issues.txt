Document: Wi-Fi 5GHz Band Issues (Error W105)
Owner: Team Member 2
============================================================

SYMPTOM
During setup the app shows Error W105: Network not supported. The device LED blinks red
slowly.

CAUSE
The phone is connected to a 5 GHz network, or the router uses a combined SSID with band
steering that forces the device onto 5 GHz. NovaTech sensors, cameras and locks cannot
join 5 GHz.

SOLUTION
1. In your router settings, create a separate 2.4 GHz SSID or temporarily disable the 5
GHz radio. 2. Connect your phone to the 2.4 GHz network. 3. Restart pairing. 4. After
successful setup, you can re-enable 5 GHz.

NOTES
Channel width should be 20 MHz and the channel should be 1, 6 or 11 for best
compatibility.

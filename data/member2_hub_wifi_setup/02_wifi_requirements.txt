NOVATECH SMART HOME - SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE
Document: Wi-Fi Requirements for NovaTech Devices
Owner: Team Member 2
============================================================

SUPPORTED STANDARDS
NovaTech devices support 802.11 b/g/n on the 2.4 GHz band. NovaHub X1 also supports 5 GHz
for its own uplink, but all connected sensors, cameras and locks use 2.4 GHz only.

MINIMUM SPEEDS
NovaCam requires at least 2 Mbps upload per camera for 1080p streaming. The hub requires 1
Mbps.

SECURITY
WPA2-Personal is supported. WPA3-only networks and enterprise (802.1X) networks are not
supported. Hidden SSIDs may require manual entry.

PASSWORD RULES
Wi-Fi passwords with unusual special characters such as accents or emoji can cause pairing
to fail.

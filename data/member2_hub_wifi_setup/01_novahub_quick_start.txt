Document: NovaHub Quick Start Guide
Owner: Team Member 2
============================================================

IN THE BOX
NovaHub X1, power adapter (5V 2A), Ethernet cable, quick start card, wall-mount screw kit.

SETUP STEPS
1. Plug in the hub and wait for the LED to pulse white. 2. Download the NovaTech app and
create an account. 3. Tap Add Device > NovaHub. 4. Scan the QR code on the bottom of the
hub. 5. Select your home Wi-Fi network and enter the password.

LED STATUS
Pulsing white: ready to pair. Solid blue: connected and online. Slow red blink: no
internet. Fast red blink: hardware fault. Pulsing amber: firmware update in progress.

PLACEMENT
Place the hub centrally, at least 1 meter from microwaves and cordless phones, and off the
floor.

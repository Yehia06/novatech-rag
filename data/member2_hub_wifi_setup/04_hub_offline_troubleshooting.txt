Document: Hub Offline Troubleshooting (Error H201)
Owner: Team Member 2
============================================================

SYMPTOM
The app shows Hub Offline or Error H201. LED is slow blinking red.

QUICK CHECKS
1. Confirm other devices have internet. 2. Power-cycle the hub by unplugging for 30
seconds. 3. Power-cycle the router.

ETHERNET FALLBACK
Connect the hub to the router using the Ethernet cable. The hub prefers a wired connection
automatically.

STILL OFFLINE
Check whether the router has MAC filtering or a firewall blocking outbound ports 443 and
8883. If unresolved after 15 minutes, perform a hub factory reset (see Reset document).

NOVATECH SMART HOME - SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE
Document: Factory Resetting the NovaHub
Owner: Team Member 2
============================================================

WHEN TO RESET
Use only if the hub is unresponsive, being sold, or after changing Wi-Fi network without
app access.

PROCEDURE
Locate the recessed Reset button on the back. With the hub powered on, hold the button
with a paper clip for 10 seconds until the LED flashes red three times. Release; the hub
restarts and pulses white.

CONSEQUENCES
All paired devices are disconnected from the hub, schedules and automations stored locally
are erased. Cloud-saved scenes can be restored after re-pairing.

REMINDER
Remove the hub from your account in the app before resetting to avoid ownership conflicts
(Error A301).

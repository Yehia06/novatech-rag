Document: Firmware Updates
Owner: Team Member 2
============================================================

AUTOMATIC UPDATES
Firmware updates install automatically between 2 AM and 4 AM local time when the hub is
online.

MANUAL UPDATES
Open Device > Settings > Firmware > Check for Updates. Updates take 5 to 10 minutes; do
not unplug during an update (amber pulsing LED).

FAILED UPDATE
Error F410 indicates a failed update. Power-cycle the hub and retry. If it fails three
times, contact support; the hub may require a recovery flash.

RELEASE NOTES
Latest version is shown in the app. Versions follow the pattern 3.x.y.

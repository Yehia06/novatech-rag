NOVATECH SMART HOME - SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE
Document: Using NovaTech Devices with Mesh Wi-Fi and Extenders
Owner: Team Member 2
============================================================

MESH NETWORKS
Mesh systems are supported if all nodes broadcast the same 2.4 GHz SSID. Disable AP
isolation and client isolation.

RANGE EXTENDERS
Extenders that create a separate SSID are not recommended. Devices may stay connected to a
weak extender; reposition or enable roaming support.

SIGNAL STRENGTH
In the app, Device > Signal shows RSSI. Values above -65 dBm are good; below -75 dBm cause
drops.

RECOMMENDATION
Place the hub closer to battery-powered devices rather than adding extenders.

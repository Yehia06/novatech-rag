NOVATECH SMART HOME - SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE
Document: Data Export and Account Deletion
Owner: Team Member 5
============================================================

EXPORT
Request a copy of personal data at Account > Privacy > Download My Data. Archives are
prepared within 72 hours as a ZIP with JSON files.

DELETION
Account > Privacy > Delete Account. Deletion is permanent after a 14-day grace period.

BEFORE DELETING
Cancel subscriptions and remove devices. Download clips you want to keep.

WARRANTY IMPACT
Deleting your account does not remove warranty rights, but the serial number must be re-
registered with proof of purchase.

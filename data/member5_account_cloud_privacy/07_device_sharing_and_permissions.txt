NOVATECH SMART HOME - SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE
Document: Device Sharing and Permissions
Owner: Team Member 5
============================================================

ROLES
Owner: full control. Manager: add devices, edit schedules. Member: control devices.
Viewer: view only.

INVITING
Account > Home Members > Invite by email. Invitations expire after 7 days.

TRANSFER OF OWNERSHIP
Owner must remove devices and the recipient adds them. Direct transfer produces Error
A301.

REMOVING ACCESS
Removing a member revokes their lock PINs within 1 minute when the hub is online.

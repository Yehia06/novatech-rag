Document: Account Creation and Login
Owner: Team Member 5
============================================================

CREATING AN ACCOUNT
Sign up with email and password or with Apple or Google. Email verification is required
within 24 hours.

PASSWORD RULES
At least 10 characters with one number and one symbol.

FORGOT PASSWORD
Use Forgot Password to receive a reset link valid for 30 minutes.

LOGIN PROBLEMS
Error A101 means invalid credentials. Error A205 means account temporarily locked after 5
failed attempts; wait 15 minutes.

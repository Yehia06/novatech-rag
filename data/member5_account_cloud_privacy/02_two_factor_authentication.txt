Document: Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
Owner: Team Member 5
============================================================

OPTIONS
Authenticator app (TOTP), SMS code, or hardware security key (FIDO2).

ENABLING
Account > Security > Two-Factor Authentication. Save the 10 backup codes shown after
setup.

LOST DEVICE
Use a backup code. Without one, identity verification takes 3 to 5 business days and
requires a photo ID and proof of purchase.

RECOMMENDATION
Authenticator app is recommended over SMS because of SIM-swap risk.

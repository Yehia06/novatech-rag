Document: Billing, Cancellation and Subscription Refunds
Owner: Team Member 5
============================================================

CANCEL ANYTIME
Cancel in Account > Subscription. Access continues until the end of the paid period.

REFUNDS
Monthly plans are non-refundable. Annual plans are refundable within 14 days of purchase
or renewal if no clips were downloaded.

FAILED PAYMENTS
Error B302 indicates a declined card. NovaTech retries 3 times over 7 days before
downgrading to Free.

RECEIPTS
Invoices are available under Account > Billing History.
